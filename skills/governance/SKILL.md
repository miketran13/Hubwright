# Governance and roles

Microsoft's information architecture guidance names the roles that make an intranet work:
**organization intranet owners**, **departmental business owners**, **IT admins**, **hub
owners**, **site owners**, **content creators** and **content consumers**. Write down who holds
each role before you build. Information architecture is a continuous process, not a project.

IT admins implement governance for **site creation and usage**. Decide who may create sites,
which templates they use and how names are formed, then back it with lifecycle policies.
