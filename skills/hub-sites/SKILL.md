# Hub sites

A hub gives a family of sites **shared navigation and brand**, **roll-up of content and search**,
and **a home destination**. A function that offers several logically different services, such as
HR, Finance or IT, usually deserves a hub.

**Limits and practical numbers (Microsoft):**
- Up to 2,000 hub sites per organization.
- Hub navigation has up to three levels. Hub-to-hub association reaches three levels.
- Showing every associated site in hub navigation: technically 500 links, practically no more
  than 100.
- The Sites web part, set to "all sites in the hub", shows up to 99 sites.
- Search across about 2,000 associated sites can have performance issues.

**Don'ts:**
- Don't create a hub only to share a theme.
- Don't associate extranet sites when you don't want guests to see the shared navigation.
- Don't link private sites in hub navigation without audience targeting.

**Do:** pick one hub naming convention (HR Hub, Finance Hub…) and apply one approach to regional
functions everywhere.
