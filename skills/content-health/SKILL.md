# Content health

An intranet is only as good as its newest page.

- Put the most important information **at the top of the page**. Most readers don't scroll to
  the bottom.
- Stay inside the limits: 2,000 lists and libraries per site collection, a 400-character path,
  and at most 50 default terms on a managed metadata column. Sync works best below 300,000 files.
- Keep news flowing on hubs and the home site. A news web part showing last year's posts tells
  people nobody is home.
